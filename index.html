ABOUT THIS HARNESS

What this tests

JANUS separates model output, evidence, decision, authorization and execution, and describes authority as distinct from capability. This harness introduces controlled adversarial changes at those boundaries and asks whether the published architecture provides enough information to determine what should happen next.

A result of INSUFFICIENT SPECIFICATION is not a failure of JANUS. It means the Orientation Edition intentionally does not disclose enough implementation or policy detail to derive the answer without inventing one.

Included challenges

  1. Authorization condition changes after approval
  2. Evidence changes after authorization
  3. Authorization expires before execution
  4. Learning attempts to expand authority
  5. Two legitimate authorities conflict
  6. Authority is revoked during execution
