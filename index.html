ABOUT THIS HARNESS

What this tests

JANUS separates model output, evidence, decision, authorization and execution, and describes authority as distinct from capability. This harness introduces controlled adversarial changes at those boundaries and asks whether the published orientation document provides enough information to determine what should happen next.

A result of INSUFFICIENT SPECIFICATION is not a failure of JANUS. It means the Orientation Edition does not disclose enough implementation or policy detail to derive a mechanism without inventing one.

Integrity limit. Evidence exports are unsigned client-side artifacts. PASS means the page's checks were internally consistent at export time; it is not a digital signature or proof against a user who controls DevTools/console state and can recompute client-side hashes.
